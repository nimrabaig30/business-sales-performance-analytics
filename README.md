# Business Sales Performance Analytics

## Project Overview
An interactive sales performance dashboard built using Microsoft Power BI for the Future Interns Data Science & Analytics Internship — Task 1.

## Tools Used
- Microsoft Power BI Desktop
- Power Query for data preparation
- DAX for measures and KPIs
- Superstore Sales Dataset

## Dashboard Features
- Key performance indicators: Total Sales, Total Profit, Total Orders, Total Quantity, and Profit Margin
- Monthly Sales Trend (2014–2017)
- Sales by Region
- Sales by Category
- Profit by Category
- Top 10 Products by Sales

## Key Business Insights
- Total sales: approximately $2.30 million.
- Total profit: approximately $286,397.
- Overall profit margin: approximately 12.47%.
- The West region generates the highest sales, followed by the East.
- Technology contributes the highest profit among the three categories.
- Furniture generates comparatively lower profit, suggesting an opportunity to review costs and discount strategies.

## Recommendations
- Investigate strategies used in high-performing regions to improve sales elsewhere.
- Review discounts and costs for low-profit products.
- Prioritize products with strong sales and healthy profit margins.
- Monitor sales and profitability monthly.
